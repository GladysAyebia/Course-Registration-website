FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our courses and services

What are the prerequisites for enrolling in a course?

Most of our courses are designed for beginners, so no prior experience is required. However, basic computer literacy and familiarity with using the internet are recommended. Specific prerequisites for advanced courses will be listed in the course description.

How long do I have access to the course materials?

Once enrolled, you have lifetime access to the course materials, including any future updates. You can learn at your own pace and revisit the content whenever you need.

Do you offer certificates upon completion?

Yes! Upon successful completion of each course, you will receive a verified certificate from SkaiTech that you can add to your resume and LinkedIn profile.

What software engineering services do you provide?

We offer a comprehensive range of services including custom software development, mobile app development, cloud solutions, data engineering, cybersecurity services, and IT consulting. Contact us for a free consultation.

Is there any job placement assistance?

Yes, we provide career support services including resume reviews, interview preparation, portfolio building, and job placement assistance to help you land your dream job in tech.
